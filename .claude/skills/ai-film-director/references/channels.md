# Channel playbooks — Persian history page and Persian kids page

Caveat: research used search snippets only; official policy pages are cited by URL but their current wording was not read directly. **Re-verify policy text before acting.**

## A. Persian history page

### Formats
- **Short (Reels/Shorts/TikTok): POV / time-traveller vlog** — "a day in Persepolis under Darius", "a merchant on the Silk Road in Nishapur". Proven viral trend (POV Lab, Time Traveller POV, Chloe VS History — [Guardian via inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/we-can-stitch-together-our-past-the-ai-generated-time-travellers-vlogging-from-history), [Hola Jun 2026](https://www.hola.com/us/lifestyle/20260626909651/ai-history-influencer-tiktok-debate/)).
- **Medium (3–8 min): explainers with animated maps** ("how the Safavid borders changed") or a **talking historical figure** monologue clearly framed as dramatisation.
- **Long (10–20 min): calm narrated story / "history to fall asleep to"** in Persian — likely under-served (no Persian competitors found). English "boring history" channels exist at scale (Sleepless Historian ~614k subs, daily multi-hour videos — [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/ai-generated-boring-history-videos-are-flooding-youtube-and-drowning-out-real-history/)) and are criticised for errors.
- Field is crowded and criticised for anachronisms (sunglasses in ancient Rome — [getcoai](https://getcoai.com/news/ai-generated-history-videos-are-going-viral-on-tiktok-but-how-accurate-are-they)). Stand out with: human-researched script with sources, a **recurring host character**, real story arc, original commentary.

### Suggested 15-min episode structure (working template)
```
0:00–0:30  Cold open: the most dramatic moment, then a question
0:30–1:30  Host intro + map: where and when
1:30–12:30 3–4 sequences (rise / conflict / turning point / consequence), each ends on a question
12:30–14:00 What it meant / what changed / how we know (sources on screen)
14:00–15:00 Final image echoing the open; next-episode tease; source card
```
Styles that fit: Persian miniature (parallax, limited animation), paper cut-out, realistic historical cinematic (needs disclosure), 2D storybook. Maps/titles: Remotion (nfp-alvana kit).

### Accuracy routine (mandatory)
- Script from named sources (Encyclopaedia Iranica, Cambridge History of Iran, …); source line in caption and on an end card.
- **No invented quotes.** AI tools fabricate dates, casualty figures and attributed quotes ([FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/history-facts-shorts-guide), low authority). Every quote must have a source; otherwise label "dramatised".
- Mark invented dialogue as dramatisation on screen.
- **Anachronism check per era** (verify each item for your period):
  - Clothing, armour, weapons (no stirrups/firearms where they didn't exist).
  - Writing script: e.g. **no Nastaliq calligraphy in the Achaemenid era** (cuneiform / Aramaic then).
  - Architecture (domes, tilework, iwans by period).
  - Foods and plants: **no tomatoes or potatoes before the Columbian exchange**; check tea, coffee, sugar, chili too.
  - Animals, coins, flags and symbols, lighting (candles/oil lamps, not electric), eyewear, paper vs parchment.
  - Faces of rulers: base on coins, reliefs, portraits (Cyrus, Darius, Shah Abbas, Nader Shah are acceptable to depict).
- Ask the user to confirm historical choices you are unsure of; never guess contested facts.

### Sensitivities (Iran)
- **Never show the faces of the Prophet Muhammad, the Imams or Ahl al-Bayt.** Use light, silhouette, a veiled face or halo, calligraphy, or an off-screen narrator — as traditional Iranian religious painting does. Shia theology is described as forbidding reproducing these faces ([Quartz](https://qz.com/568430/irans-whimsical-censorship-system-made-one-religious-film-a-blockbuster-while-banning-another)); *Rastakhiz* (2014) was pulled on day one over the depiction of Abbas despite 40 min of cuts ([Iran Human Rights](https://iranhumanrights.org/?p=20509)). Iran's 1983 film code bans insult to Prophets, Imams and the Supreme Leader ([La Vie des idées PDF](https://laviedesidees.fr/IMG/pdf/20260519_irancine.pdf)).
- **Do not make modern political figures speak through AI** (deepfake risk; political topics are sensitive on YouTube and in Iran).
- Avoid "AI historian giving political advice" framing — YouTube category 3 below.

### Disclosure
- YouTube: tick **altered/synthetic** for realistic reenactments (free; doesn't limit reach). Fully animated / clearly unrealistic content is exempt ([YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)).
- Instagram/Meta: "AI info" label, auto-detected from C2PA/IPTC metadata; may be required for photorealistic video or realistic audio ([Meta Help](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/1783222608822690/)). Flow/Veo outputs carry SynthID + C2PA (visible watermark optional since Aug 2026, metadata stays — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-google-gemini-visible-watermarks-optional/)).
- Add English subtitles too for second-generation diaspora and non-Persian viewers.

## B. Persian kids page

### Context (why quality matters)
Kids' AI content is the most scrutinised category of 2026: NYT investigation into AI "educational" slop for toddlers, 5 channels suspended from YPP ([Digital Trends](https://digitaltrends.com/social-media/investigation-finds-youtube-is-serving-mindless-ai-slop-to-toddlers-and-preschoolers)); 200+ organisations asked YouTube to ban AI from YouTube Kids; YouTube said it limits AI content on YouTube Kids to "a small set of high-quality channels" ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/01/ai-slop-200-organizations-letter-youtube-google/)). Expect to grow on main YouTube (made for kids) and Instagram first.

### Formats by age band (inference)
- **2–5:** songs, nursery rhymes, Persian lullabies (laalaayi), counting, colours, alphabet — 2–4 min.
- **4–7:** short stories with a moral — Kelileh-o-Demneh, folk tales (Khaleh Sooskeh, Kadoo Ghelgheleh Zan), Mollah Nasreddin — 3–7 min.
- **6–10:** explainers (animals, science, Nowruz traditions) — 4–8 min.
- **10–20 min program:** compile 3–4 segments with a recurring host character and a song bridge, or one longer story in chapters. Note AAP: ages 2–5 at most 1 h/day of high-quality programming, ideally co-viewed ([NPR 2016](https://www.npr.org/sections/ed/2016/10/21/498550475/american-academy-of-pediatrics-lifts-no-screens-under-2-rule)).

### Pacing & craft for kids (calm, slower)
- Fast-paced cartoons hurt 4-year-olds' executive function right after viewing (Lillard & Peterson 2011, [NPR](https://www.npr.org/sections/health-shots/2011/09/12/140401099/spongebob-may-be-too-speedy-for-preschool-brains)); later work is mixed — fantastical content may matter more than pace ([BMC Psychology 2024](https://bmcpsychology.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s40359-024-01738-1/tables/2)). Experts criticise CoComelon's pace and shallow content ([AAP Q&A](https://www.aap.org/en/patient-care/media-and-children/center-of-excellence-on-social-media-and-youth-mental-health/qa-portal/qa-portal-library/qa-portal-library-questions/watching-cocomelon-and-guidance-on-early-childhood-development/)).
- Hold shots **5–8 s**, not 1–3 s; few zooms and whip-pans; slow push-ins at most.
- Calm palette, not constantly saturated; soft light.
- Clear, slow, warm speech; repetition for learning with **pauses for the child to answer**.
- Coherent narrative, consistent characters, **no bizarre AI morphing**; model safe, kind behaviour.
- **Human review of every frame** before upload (extra fingers, morphing faces erode parents' trust).
- Stylized characters only; never real child photos as references (safety filters block children — codes reported for Child / Celebrity — [forum](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/veo-3-1-blocking-images-of-our-own-ai-avatar-generated-using-nano-banana-pro/114368)). Describe style by properties, not "Pixar".
- **Original or public-domain folk characters only** — no Peppa, Bluey, Disney, Shrek lookalikes (general IP principle).

### Hijab and modesty
Khamenei ruled women in **Iranian-made** cartoons must wear hijab ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/irans-supreme-leader-says-women-in-cartoons-must-wear-hijabs/)). For creators/audiences inside Iran, modest dress / hijab for female characters avoids friction; for a diaspora-only audience it's the user's choice (inference). Ask the user once and record the decision in the character bible.

### YouTube "made for kids" / COPPA
- Content aimed at under-13s **must** be set "made for kids": disables personalised ads, comments, notifications and some features. Set at channel level; if the channel is "not made for kids", each kids video must be set individually ([FTC 2025](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2025/09/protecting-children-watching-youtube-videos-lessons-learned-ftcs-settlement-disney)).
- Mislabelling cost Disney $10M (Sept 2025); YouTube/Google paid $170M in 2019 ([FTC 2019](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2019/11/youtube-channel-owners-your-content-directed-children)).
- YouTube quality principles: promoted = high-quality, educational, positive; limited/no ads for heavily commercial content, negative behaviours, "deceptively educational" content ([YouTube Help 10774223](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10774223?hl=en)).
- Animated AI is exempt from YouTube's synthetic-content label, but voluntary disclosure builds parents' trust.

## C. Monetisation risk: YouTube "inauthentic content" (both channels)
- Renamed from "repetitious" on 2025-07-15; applies to the **whole channel**; using AI is not by itself disqualifying ([YPP policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)).
- July 2026 clarification — not monetisable ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/)):
  1. **Generic or repetitive:** low educational value, little commentary, near-identical videos, **the same characters in the same situation over and over**.
  2. **Unsatisfying or off-putting:** disturbing themes without cohesive narrative, animal-peril loops, AI clips stitched without narrative logic.
  3. **AI personas on sensitive topics:** AI presented as a human expert on health, legal, finance, politics.
- Enforcement: 16 channels / 35M subs terminated Jan 2026 ([TheNextWeb](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage?type=AI)).
- **Defences:** real story with an arc; vary characters, situations, locations and structure across episodes; original research/commentary; human voice or human-written script; no template reused across hundreds of uploads.
- **Iran audience:** YouTube now classes much VPN Persian traffic as in-Iran, where sanctions mean no ad revenue ([NIAC](https://insights.niacouncil.org/p/when-sanctions-silence-iranian-voices)). Target diaspora (Canada, USA, Germany, UK) for ads; use Instagram for reach (Instagram is the main home for Persian creators — [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/in-depth-research-reports/report/iranians-on-socialmedia/)); diversify (sponsors, memberships, story/song packs).

## D. Persian voice options
- **ElevenLabs** (multilingual; markets Persian TTS, "Eleven v4" advertised as most expressive — [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/text-to-speech/persian), vendor claim). Likely best quality; no independent benchmark. In this kit: `tools/eleven-*` with `--env-file=.env` (key is a network secret).
- **Azure**: 2 Persian neural voices (fa-IR), SSML, free F0 tier ([json2video](https://json2video.com/ai-voices/azure/languages/persian/), [VideoSDK](https://www.videosdk.live/developer-hub/tts/persian-text-to-speech)).
- **Google Cloud TTS**: Persian Standard and WaveNet ([voices](https://docs.cloud.google.com/text-to-speech/docs/voices)).
- **The user's own recorded voice** — most trusted for kids, strongest "authentic" signal.
- Research baseline: Persian TTS MOS ~3.7–4.1 ([arXiv 2510.10774](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.10774v2)).
- Routine: A/B the same test sentences (ezafe, numbers, Arabic loanwords, names) across voices; build a **pronunciation list** for names (Xšayāršā, Khwarazm, Ghaznavid); kids = warm, slow voice.
- Subtitles: burn in Persian (RTL, Vazirmatn) — most viewers watch muted.
